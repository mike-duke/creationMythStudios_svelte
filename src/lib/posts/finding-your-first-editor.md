Hiring an editor for the first time can feel a little like handing someone your
diary. Here's how to make it less nerve-wracking and more productive.

## Start with a sample

Any editor worth working with will offer a short sample edit. This is the single
best way to see how someone reads your work before you commit to a full project.

## Ask about process

- How do they deliver notes?
- What's their turnaround?
- Do they specialize in your genre?

## Trust the fit

An edit is a working relationship. If the sample feels encouraging *and*
honest — the kind of feedback that makes you want to get back to the page —
that's the one.
