Before you send a manuscript to an editor (or an agent), a focused self-edit can
save you time and money. Here's a short checklist I share with clients.

## Read it out loud

Your ear catches what your eye skips: clunky rhythm, repeated words, dialogue
that no human would say.

## Hunt your crutch words

We all have them — *just*, *really*, *suddenly*, *began to*. Search for yours and
cut what you don't need.

## Check your openings

Read the first line of every chapter in a row. Do they vary? Do they pull you
forward? Openings set the pace for everything after.

Do these three passes and your draft will already read cleaner — and your editor
can spend their time on the deeper work.
