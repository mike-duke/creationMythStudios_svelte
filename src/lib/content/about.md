## About me

I'm a freelance fiction editor working with novelists and short-story writers
across genres. Before going independent I spent years reading slush, coaching
writers through revisions, and learning what actually makes a draft click into
place.

I believe editing is a conversation, not a verdict. My job isn't to rewrite your
book in my voice — it's to help you find the strongest version of your own. That
means asking the right questions, flagging what pulls a reader out of the story,
and celebrating what's already working so you know what to protect.

### How I work

- **A sample first.** Before any paid project, I'll edit a short sample so we can
  make sure we're a good fit.
- **Clear notes.** You'll get tracked changes plus an editorial letter that
  explains the *why* behind big-picture suggestions.
- **Your voice, protected.** I edit toward your intentions, not a house style.

When I'm not editing, I'm reading widely, over-annotating library books, and
quietly rooting for your characters.
